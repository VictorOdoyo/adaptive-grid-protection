# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Coupled phase-network fault constraints, grounded conventional sources,
  independent sequence references, power balance and post-trip connectivity.
- Voltage-dependent per-phase-limited inverter currents and explicit sequence policy.
- Sampled feeder feedback with Fourier measurement, CT/ADC/filter effects,
  directional memory, inverse-time travel, breaker failure and isolation.
- Overcurrent, distance, directional and differential element libraries with
  validity, harmonic, load-encroachment, freshness and skew supervision.
- Audited continuous/discrete and sampled-robust coordination; immutable adaptive
  groups with exact envelope certificates and guarded atomic transitions.
- Fault-location, off-nominal estimation, CT energy, pilot-channel, reclosure
  and statistical diagnostic helpers.
- Twenty-seven reproducible studies, including deliberately retained missed and
  sympathetic operations, with sealed arrays, CSV/JSON, figures and HTML reports.
- Primary-unit CSV import/export and source-linked offline relay replay.
- Linux/Windows numerical checks, pinned CI actions and isolated wheel installation.

The feeder is a phasor-envelope/sampled-waveform model. Full switching EMT,
manufacturer validation, standards acceptance and field commissioning are outside
this release.
